From phone to pen in three steps

  1. Connect the pen. Plug the pen directly into your phone. Open Pen Library, tap Connect a pen, and grant Android access to the pen’s top-level folder.
  2. Find your book. Open Browse books and search the German catalog for the title. Pick the edition that matches your book and download its audio.
  3. Install and try the book. Open the downloaded book and tap Install on pen. Keep the app open and the pen connected until the transfer is verified. Unplug it and tap your book with the pen.

Will my pen work?

You need Android 8 or later, USB storage support (OTG) and a pen that shows up as a USB drive. Pen Library has been tested with a fourth-generation pen. Use the pen’s USB data cable with a suitable adapter if needed.

Android can’t see the pen

Check that the cable supports data. Connect the pen directly to the phone, without the charging dock. Try another data cable or adapter. When Android asks for access, select the pen drive’s top-level folder.

Can I use it offline?

Use an internet connection to download audio. Keep the download on the phone to install it later offline. Choose Transfer only in Phone storage to download and install in one step with a temporary copy.

Can I manage more than one pen?

Connect each pen and give it a name. Pen Library stores its last-seen inventory on the phone. You can save a selection of books for your family and review which titles to add to each connected pen.

Can I free up space on the pen?

Remove a book from its detail screen. Pen Library verifies a backup on your phone first. Use Downloads to restore that copy offline. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases local downloads and backups.
